About the Lounge

Artistry, Elegance & A Personal Touch

Asiya Beauty Lounge is a premium beauty destination in the heart of Gulberg III, Lahore. Founded on Asiya’s signature makeup style, the lounge has become a favourite for brides and party-goers who want to look effortlessly radiant.
